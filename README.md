## 🇨🇳 中文

由 2 个技能组成的学术写作与评审套件，一个负责诊断，一个负责动笔。academic-evaluator 用资深审稿人和导师的眼光对科研工作做只看不改的诊断：既可以评判研究想法值不值得做，给出打分、新颖性核查与可行性判断，也可以对论文成稿挑硬伤、判断能不能投，并给出修改方向，但不替你写正文、不替你画图。academic-polish 是论文正文写作、结构设计与语言润色的总入口：完整起草、续写、扩写、补写或实质性修订中英文论文时进入 paper-write-zh 或 paper-write-en，只做提纲、结构诊断或重排方案时进入结构模式，只做忠实润色或不新增研究内容的中译英时进入润色模式。两者配合使用：先由评估技能判断选题与稿件的生死，再由写作技能按诊断结论改稿，各管一段，互不越界。

> ⚠️ **这是一套配合使用的技能组合（2 个）** —— 正文互相引用，建议成套取用。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 想法评估：给研究想法打分、查新颖性、判可行性，回答“值不值得做”
- 论文评审：投稿前挑硬伤、判断能不能投，只给问题、结论与修改方向
- 写作入口：完整起草、续写、扩写、补写或实质性修订各学科中英文论文正文
- 结构模式：只做提纲、结构诊断与章节重排方案，不写正文内容
- 润色模式：忠实润色与不新增研究内容的中译英，常见医学学术表达
- 协作边界：评估只找问题不改稿，写作只按诊断动笔，两条线接力使用

## 🇬🇧 English

Two skills for academic writing and review — one diagnoses, one does the writing. academic-evaluator reads research work the way a senior reviewer or supervisor would and only reports: it judges whether a research idea is worth pursuing with scores, novelty checks and feasibility calls, and it reviews finished manuscripts for fatal flaws and submission readiness while giving revision directions, without writing the body text or drawing figures for you. academic-polish is the main entry for paper drafting, structure design and language polishing: full drafting, continuation, expansion, backfill or substantive revision of Chinese and English papers routes to paper-write-zh or paper-write-en, outline-only or structural diagnosis and reordering routes to the structure mode, and faithful polishing or Chinese-to-English translation that adds no research content routes to the polish mode. The two are meant to be paired: evaluate first to settle the idea and the draft, then write to the diagnosis.

> ⚠️ **A cooperating suite of 2 skills** — they reference each other; use them as a group.

### ✨ What it does

- Idea evaluation: score a research idea, check novelty and feasibility, and answer whether it is worth doing
- Manuscript review: find fatal flaws before submission, call whether it can be submitted, and give problems, verdict and revision directions only
- Writing entry: full drafting, continuation, expansion, backfill or substantive revision of Chinese and English paper bodies across disciplines
- Structure mode: outline, structural diagnosis and section-reordering plans only, with no body prose
- Polish mode: faithful polishing and Chinese-to-English translation that adds no research content
- Clear boundary: evaluation only finds problems, writing only acts on the diagnosis — the two run in sequence
